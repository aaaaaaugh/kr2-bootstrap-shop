About Our Platform

We are an open, independent music streaming platform designed for indie creators and passionate music lovers. Our mission is to directly connect artists with listeners worldwide.

Creative Freedom

Upload tracks across diverse genres: Ambient, Lo-Fi, Electronic, Hip-Hop, Rock, and Pop.

Our Mission

We believe great music doesn't require multi-million dollar studio budgets. The most authentic tracks are often born right at home. SoundProject levels the playing field so every artist gets a fair chance to be heard.

Listen to tracks ad-free, build your personal library, and support upcoming musicians in reaching new audiences!
